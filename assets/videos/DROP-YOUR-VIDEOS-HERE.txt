HOW TO ADD YOUR VIDEOS
======================

Drop your video files (.mp4 / .mov) into the matching folder:

  ugc/         -> UGC Ads
  hype/        -> HYPE Ads & Animations
  podcast-ai/  -> Podcast & AI Ads
  vsl/         -> Video Sales Letters
  voiceover/   -> Voiceover & Bilingual Ads

Then tell Claude "videos are in" — they will be compressed for web
and wired into the site automatically. Original files are never
modified or deleted.
